In your notebook

A note for your grown-up

Read the short story together. One dot or box stands for one thing; there’s no need to copy the whole picture. Let your child count, draw and write before revealing the answer. If the answers differ, count the marks together. There’s no timer or score.

Adding means joining more to a group. Taking away means crossing some out and counting what remains. Start with “Up to 5”; “Up to 10” also includes a story with nothing left.
