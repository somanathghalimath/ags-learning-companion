Kannada handwriting: shape and stroke review

15 swaras and 34 vyanjanas. Each numbered panel represents one uninterrupted pen-down movement. Green dots mark starts; orange arrows show travel direction. Blue lines are earlier strokes, green is the current stroke, grey is still to come. This is a source-grounded revision, not a Kannada teacher certification.
