# Kannada handwriting research and revision

Research date: 31 October 2026. Scope: the 15 existing Kannada learning characters in Write With Me. This revision improves centreline letter construction and the demonstration of movement; it does not add a new teaching syllabus.

## What is established, and what is not

A finished printed letter is not evidence of pen order. The movement convention implemented here is the one demonstrated in Gopala Krishna A's handwriting animations. School copybooks and official primers are used to check recognisability, component attachment and finished shapes. They are not treated as stroke-order authorities when they show no directional information.

This is a source-grounded reconstruction, not an independent certification by a Kannada handwriting teacher. Different teachers may use different legitimate pen-lift conventions. The application demonstrates one documented convention consistently; it does not label other conventions wrong. The child should not be taught a newly invented path merely to imitate a font outline.

### Access limitations

KTBS's linked Nali Kali textbook and DSERT worksheet URLs timed out. The older Kannada Abhivruddhi Pradhikara PDF URL hosted by HaKKa returned an HTML homepage rather than the book. Teacher video links could not be played (one tested download reported unavailable). Search excerpts mentioning alternate two-stroke descriptions of ಔ were not enough to settle a school-wide standard, so they were not treated as verified instructions. These unavailable materials are not counted as reviewed books or videos.

## What changed

- Refit the 15 old raster-derived centreline vectors with shorter smooth cubic curves. The fitting pass constrains ordered source-sample deviation to two SVG units before the explicit repairs listed below. The original traversal order and endpoints are otherwise retained.
- Repaired the missing left arc of the anusvara in ಅಂ. The old path stopped approximately 48 SVG units from its start and taught an open C-like mark; the reference finishes a closed circle.
- Removed an artificial pen lift within ಔ's final spiral. The legacy third path ended at precisely the fourth path's start. Those two pieces now form one uninterrupted stroke. The short head bar also no longer doubles back over itself because of trace noise.
- Restored junctions in ಈ, ಋ and ಓ where the old trace stopped short of visible source connections. The additions are reconstruction decisions made from the final reference forms; they are called out rather than misrepresented as exact source coordinates. The small finishing crest on ಋ is restored as well.
- Reduced the monoline pen width from 18 to 12 viewBox units to expose counters and keep distinct loops visible at phone size. These are pencil-centreline demonstrations, not facsimiles of the source's broad-nib thick/thin calligraphy.
- Before each Kannada pen-down movement, show its start dot for 800 ms. Show an explicit lift cue only between separate strokes. Pause preserves the pencil position in Kannada.
- Use constant distance-based motion for Kannada, rather than accelerating at the beginning and slowing to a crawl at the end. Long continuous paths are no longer capped to the same maximum animation duration.
- Display the current movement cue and stroke number. The final grey guide and blue ink use the **same** paths, so the displayed letter cannot silently switch to a different font at completion.

The English uppercase, lowercase and number content and their existing movement behaviour remain unchanged. No runtime fonts, OCR, image tracing or numerical libraries were added.

## Review and validation

Open [kannada-review.html](kannada-review.html) for completed forms, numbered stroke panels, green starting points, direction arrows and playback. [before-after.png](before-after.png) compares the previous and revised forms. Review the attachments in ಈ/ಋ/ಓ and the corrected ಔ/ಅಂ particularly closely.

Automated browser checks exercise all 15 characters at Slow, Normal and Fast; 800 ms preparation; constant path-distance motion; pen-lift boundaries; completed solid ink; pause/resume/replay; and mobile/tablet/desktop layouts. English and number character data are compared with the previous committed data. These checks can verify rendering and animation behaviour; they cannot certify handwriting pedagogy. The source comparison and the reconstruction choices above remain available for parent/teacher review.
